Do not alter the original source or the restored raw copy.

Store raw values and normalized values separately. Use stable generated keys for split child records.

## Preparation blockers discovered locally

- City districts are absent. A city/district crosswalk is required.
- Three parties disagree with their referenced city's state. Confirm the intended location instead of choosing one silently.
- Four contacts have blank first address lines. Required destination address values need review.
- Source contact types say Creditors/Debtors, while invoice customers predominantly use the first type. Preserve observed meaning until reviewed.
- Company/year separation and inconsistent period dates require an explicit mapping decision.
- All invoice active flags are set. Lock flags vary, but neither proves target confirmation.
- Four quantity strings contain whitespace or a trailing decimal point. Lossless normalization is possible, but must retain raw values.
- Invoice source key 973 has item taxable total 1120.00 versus header subtotal 1059.20. Hold its entire aggregate pending a business decision.
- Twenty-one invoices differ between saved CGST/SGST totals and a sum of per-line rounded calculations.
  Rounding tax once per document reduces this to five invoices. The largest per-line comparison difference is 5.47 per component.
  Determine the historical formula and review the remaining differences before acceptance. Do not distribute differences arbitrarily across lines.
- Header rounding uses an unsigned magnitude. Deriving the signed residual reconciles every header within 0.50.
- Most invoices contain a nonzero referred/settled aggregate. Receipt tables exist, but receipts and allocations are outside the four-table request.
- Old strings use `latin1_swedish_ci`. Verify decoded text before UTF-8 staging. Do not blindly reinterpret bytes or lose characters.

## Local preparation implementation

The harness is `tools/e2e/data-bridge-legacy-prepare.ts`. Run Core, then Masters, then Billing.
Use `CODEXSUN_BRIDGE_LEGACY_PREPARE=local-docker-only`, loopback DB_HOST, and Docker port 13306.
Credentials stay in process environment. Never paste them into commands, reports, or Git.
The source is the isolated raw restore. Fresh target/staging names use a guarded local prefix.

- `--phase=core` creates fresh schemas with tenant-runtime and Core owner migrations/seeds, then prepares Common records.
- `--phase=core-refresh` rebuilds Core review metadata before Masters. It never updates the source or business target.
- `--phase=masters` loads the Core crosswalk and prepares company/year candidates, contacts/addresses/channels, and products.
- `--phase=billing` applies Billing owner migrations/seeds, then prepares every invoice and every item with parent links.
- `--phase=billing-refresh` rebuilds Billing review records only when no invoice has been materialized. It preserves Core and Masters preparation.
- `--phase=report` regenerates the complete private lists and field inventory without importing business data.
