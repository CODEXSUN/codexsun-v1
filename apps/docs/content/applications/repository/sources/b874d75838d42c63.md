# C10 - Marketplace merchant-of-record, tax, invoice issuer, and settlement

Status: decision brief, human business/legal decision required. Blocks EC-T13. No settlement code may
be written before this decision.

## Existing behavior

- Ecommerce has no marketplace vendor offers, suborders, commission, payout, or settlement ledger.
- Billing has sales invoices and receipts only; no credit note, refund, or payout document.
- Platform has no vendor principal (C02) and no payment-provider payout contract.
- EC-D07 records the marketplace as blocked. EC-C10 is `MISSING`.

## Exact gap

The repository cannot answer who sells to the customer, who issues the tax invoice, who remits tax, who
bears fraud/chargeback loss, and how a vendor is paid. Implementing any one answer implicitly chooses a
legal and financial model, so this is not a routine technical choice.

## Options with consequences

### Option A - Ecommerce is merchant of record (single seller)

- One invoice from the tenant company; vendor is a supplier with a commission/purchase relationship.
- Tax, refunds, chargebacks, and customer communication stay with the tenant.
- Vendor settlement is a payable: commission retained, remainder paid on a schedule.
- Lowest customer confusion and one Billing document chain; highest working-capital and compliance load.
- Needs: vendor payable document, commission tax treatment, payout approval, negative-balance policy.

### Option B - Vendor is merchant of record (marketplace)

- Each vendor issues its own invoice; Ecommerce is an intermediary/agent.
- Tenant may need commission invoices to vendors and marketplace facilitator tax treatment.
- Refund and chargeback responsibility split by vendor; customer sees multiple invoices.
- Needs: vendor principal, vendor tax identity, per-vendor invoice sequencing, facilitator rules,
  settlement and reserve policy. Much larger legal and reconciliation surface.

### Option C - Hybrid by category or geography

- Some lines sold by the tenant, some by vendors.
- Requires per-line seller identity, split invoices, split tax, and split settlement.
- Only viable after A or B is proven; highest implementation and audit cost.

## Decision questions (answer all before EC-T13)

1. Which entity is the seller of record for each line?
2. Who issues the tax invoice, and under which GSTIN/place of supply?
3. Who bears chargebacks, fraud, and return shipping?
4. When is vendor payable recognized: order, shipment, delivery, or return window close?
5. What reserve/holdback and payout schedule apply?
6. Are vendor payouts in Billing, a payout provider, or both?
7. What is the commission tax treatment (fee invoice, net settlement, or self-billing)?

## Recommended path

Start with Option A and a single-company catalog. Add vendor offers only as a supplier/commission
model after C02 and C03 are accepted. Defer true multi-vendor invoicing until legal confirms Option B
or C. This preserves one invoice chain and keeps Ecommerce out of a second financial ledger.

## Compatibility and tests (after decision)

- No Ecommerce table may store a financial balance that duplicates Billing payables.
- Every settlement row links to an accepted Billing document UUID (`CHAR(8)`) and a vendor membership
  (`app_portal_memberships.id`, `INT`). Billing and Core references stay as the owning tenant's
  integer IDs; do not invent a commerce UUID type.
- Regression: duplicate settlement run is idempotent; commission and tax totals reconcile; cross-tenant
  vendor isolation; refund reduces payable exactly once; no payout without an approved settlement.

## Repository identity conformance

- Settlement and offer rows use `id INT NOT NULL AUTO_INCREMENT` and `uuid CHAR(8)` lowercase hex,
  matching Ecommerce tables. No `BIGINT` or `CHAR(36)` identity is introduced.
- Statuses use `VARCHAR(24/32)` values validated by the owner; no MySQL `ENUM`.
- Billing documents are `billing_sales.uuid` / `billing_receipts.uuid` (`CHAR(8)`), and vendor
  membership is the C02 tenant-local `app_portal_memberships` row (`INT` id, `CHAR(8)` uuid).
- Tenant identity is the selected tenant database; settlement rows must not copy Core contact/company
  data or global tenant selectors.

## Unblock path

Business owner selects an option and answers the seven questions. Supervisor then splits EC-T13 into
vendor identity, offer/suborder, commission calculation, Billing handoff extension, and settlement
reporting, each with its own acceptance gate.
